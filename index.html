Safety

Celebrate safely

  • Light one cracker at a time, at arm's length, with the fuse tip pointing away from you.
  • Keep a bucket of water or sand close by.
  • Use an open area away from houses, vehicles and dry leaves.
  • Children should light crackers only with an adult watching.
  • Never relight a cracker that did not go off. Pour water on it and leave it.
  • Wear cotton clothes and step back right after lighting.
